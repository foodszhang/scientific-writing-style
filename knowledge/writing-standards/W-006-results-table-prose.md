# W-006 — Results prose should interpret table patterns rather than transcribe cells

**Status:** ADOPTED  
**Category:** Results / Figure/Table

## Original comment

Results paragraphs became long because they repeated most values already visible in a table.

## Trigger example

A paragraph restates every metric for every method immediately below the table.

## Underlying concern

The prose adds little information and obscures the scientific comparison. The reader needs the pattern, contrast, exception, or tested question more than a verbal copy of the table.

## External verification

### Official guidance

No explicit IEEE/TMI prohibition identified.

### Recent-paper evidence

Formal paper sampling is pending. The rule is retained as active editorial guidance because it improves information density without removing evidence.

## Assessment

**Judgment:** Strongly supported

## Current rule

When a table already reports the full numeric matrix, Results prose should identify the main comparison, pattern, exception, uncertainty, or scope. Repeat only the values needed to substantiate the specific claim being discussed.

## Examples

### Avoid

> Method A achieved 0.71 Dice, 0.42 mm ASSD, and 0.81 mm HD95, whereas Method B achieved ...

### Prefer

> Method A improved overlap while also reducing both surface-distance metrics relative to the comparator; the largest separation was observed in HD95.
