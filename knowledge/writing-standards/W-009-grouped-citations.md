# W-009 — Grouped citation ranges are acceptable only when all cited works support the grouped statement

**Status:** CONDITIONAL  
**Category:** Citation

## Original comment

Large citation ranges can read as citation dumping when the relationship between the cited works and the sentence is unclear.

## Trigger example

> Many learning-based methods have been proposed [4]–[12].

## Underlying concern

The citation group may be formally true but rhetorically uninformative. The reader cannot tell which papers are representative, how they relate, or whether all support the same proposition.

## External verification

### Official guidance

Grouped citation ranges are not inherently incorrect.

### Recent-paper evidence

Formal sampling is pending. Grouped citations are common when several papers support one narrow background statement, but they are weaker when the sentence carries a detailed methodological or evaluative claim.

## Assessment

**Judgment:** Context-dependent

## Current rule

Use grouped citation ranges for genuinely shared, low-granularity claims. When the sentence distinguishes mechanisms, assumptions, chronology, performance, or limitations, partition the citations or name representative works so that support remains traceable.

## Examples

### Acceptable

> Deep-learning methods have also been investigated for fluorescence reconstruction [4]–[8].

when all cited papers clearly fit that category.

### Prefer more specific grouping

> Encoder–decoder approaches learn a direct measurement-to-volume mapping [4]–[6], whereas physics-guided methods retain an explicit forward-model component [7], [8].
