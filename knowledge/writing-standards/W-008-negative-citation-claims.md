# W-008 — Negative literature claims must not be attached indiscriminately to grouped citations

**Status:** ADOPTED  
**Category:** Citation / Claim Strength

## Original comment

Broad negative statements about "existing methods" were followed by large citation ranges even though the cited papers did not all make or support the same limitation claim.

## Trigger example

> Existing approaches cannot preserve fine spatial details [8]–[15].

## Underlying concern

The sentence attributes one negative property to every cited work. A grouped citation cannot safely support a universal criticism unless each cited work actually falls within that claim.

## External verification

### Official guidance

No special TMI rule is needed; this is primarily a source-support and claim-scope issue.

### Recent-paper evidence

No additional sampling is required to adopt the source-traceability principle.

## Assessment

**Judgment:** Strongly supported

## Current rule

Do not attach a broad negative claim to a citation group unless every cited source actually supports that characterization. Narrow the claim, partition the citations by limitation, or describe the specific assumption/setting in which the limitation occurs.

## Examples

### Avoid

> Existing approaches cannot exploit cross-view consistency [8]–[15].

### Prefer

> Several methods process views independently [8]–[11], while others aggregate view-level features only after per-view encoding [12]–[15].
