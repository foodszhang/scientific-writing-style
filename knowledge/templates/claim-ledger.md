## Rules

- Record what the paper actually supports, not what would be convenient to cite.
- Preserve population, modality, wavelength, geometry, and other scope conditions when they matter.
- Distinguish author interpretation from direct numerical result.
- If two papers disagree, keep both claims and note the conflict.
- A later manuscript citation should inherit the original evidence boundary.
