## Entry guidance

For each equation:

1. preserve the mathematical form needed for scientific use;
2. define every non-obvious symbol;
3. state the assumptions under which the relation is valid;
4. distinguish a forward model, approximation, regularizer, loss, diagnostic statistic, and empirical fit;
5. record whether the project uses it directly or only as a reference;
6. record what scientific conclusion may be drawn from it;
7. record what conclusion is **not** justified.
